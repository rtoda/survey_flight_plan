Download:
Everything is calculated in your browser. The coordinates you type are not sent to any server and are kept only in this browser's storage. Turning on a basemap is the one exception: it requests map tiles for the area from Esri. The ⊕ Pick buttons turn the basemap on, because a click needs a map to aim at. Fire hotspots asks Esri for NASA FIRMS detections across a region hundreds of kilometres wide, and TFRs loads the FAA's national list; neither sends the survey area. TFR outlines are for awareness only and are not a substitute for a NOTAM briefing.
